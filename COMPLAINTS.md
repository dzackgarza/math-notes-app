# Complaints

Open usability defects in the deployed web app. Each entry states the defect and the required state.
Delete an entry when the deployed app has the required state.

## The library sidebar is light

The sidebar is light gray beside the dark library, and its "Math Notes" title
is white on that gray. Required: the sidebar has the dark bar color, and the
title, the "Tags" heading, and the tag counts are legible.

## Secondary text in the creation sheets is illegible

"You can move this notebook later." and the summary line of a starting
template are dark gray on the dark sheet. Required: both have the dark-theme
secondary text color.

## New Notebook: the cover colors are words

Blue, Green, Purple, and Peach are text buttons with no heading. Required: a
"Cover Color" heading and one swatch for each color, with a ring on the
selected swatch.

## Creation sheets: unlabeled and centered controls

The location ("My Notes") and "Change Notebook" are centered text links below
left-aligned headings. The "Settings name" field in New Note has no heading.
Required: both links start at the left edge of the form, and the field is
below a "Starting Template" heading.

## Card menus open at the bottom of the screen

The **⋯** button of a notebook card or a note card opens an action sheet
across the bottom of the screen. **Sort** and **Settings** open pull-down
menus at their buttons. Required: **⋯** opens a pull-down menu at the button.
